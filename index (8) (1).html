Introduction

Welcome to my Field Study 1 and Field Study 2 E-Portfolio! This digital compilation serves as a comprehensive record of my experiential learning journey, pedagogical growth, and professional evolution as a pre-service science teacher. Housed under the College of Innovative Learning and Teaching at Taguig City University, this portfolio captures the vital bridge between educational theories learned in the classroom and their real-world applications in the dynamic learning environment of our cooperating school.

Throughout my Field Study journey, this e-portfolio documents my progression from systematic classroom observations and learner behavior analysis (FS 1) to active participation, instructional material development, and teaching assistance (FS 2). Inside these pages, you will find weekly documentations, reflective journals, instructional artifacts, and professional development milestones that reflect my commitment to fostering inquiry-based, inclusive, and engaging science education.

Ultimately, this portfolio is more than just a course requirement. It is a testament to my dedication to lifelong learning, my readiness for pre-service teaching deployment, and my aspiration to shape scientifically literate and critical-thinking minds of tomorrow.

Brief Biography

I am Princess Joy C. Nazareno, a science education major at Taguig City University. For me, teaching science isn’t just about discussing theories. It’s about turning lessons into exciting, hands-on discoveries for students. Through my Field Study journey, I am pouring my heart into learning how to design engaging lessons, connect with learners, and grow into the kind of compassionate and inspiring educator I’ve always aspired to be.

Personal Educational Philosophy

My educational philosophy is rooted in nurturing natural curiosity through science and instilling valuable life lessons. I believe that every student possesses unique potential that requires patient guidance and encouragement. As their teacher, it is my responsibility to inspire them, steer them toward the right path, and remind them that education is a continuous journey of growth and personal excellence.

Career Objectives

To successfully pass the Licensure Examination for Teachers (LET) and earn my professional license, marking the true beginning of my journey as an educator. My objective is to go beyond traditional textbook lessons by imparting meaningful life lessons, serving as a positive role model, and guiding my students along a pathway of continuous growth, self-improvement, and holistic success.

Learning Goals

As I go through my Field Study 1 and Field Study 2 journey, here are my personal learning goals and expectations.

Field Study 1

Observation and Participation

Goals

I aim to immerse myself in real classroom environments, closely observe how students behave, and see firsthand how teaching theories are actually applied in live science classes.

Expectations

I expect to gain a realistic and deep understanding of classroom dynamics, student diversity, and the everyday environment of a secondary science classroom.

Field Study 2

Participation and Teaching Assistantship

Goals

I want to put my learning into action by helping out with lesson planning, creating creative instructional materials, and trying out hands-on science teaching strategies.

Expectations

I expect to step out of my comfort zone, build my confidence in teaching delivery, and learn closely from my cooperating teacher’s mentorship.

Overall Personal Expectation

Ultimately, my goal and expectation combined is to successfully bridge educational theories with real classroom practices, build solid classroom management skills, and grow into the kind of caring, effective, and inspiring science teacher my future students can truly look up to.

Cooperating School Profile

School Profile

Taguig National High School (TNHS) is a public secondary school located along Imelda Romualdez Marcos Road, corner A. Reyes Street, Barangay New Lower Bicutan, Taguig City. It was established on January 8, 1980, and was formerly known as Fort Bonifacio College Annex before becoming Taguig Municipal High School. In 1994, it adopted its present name, Taguig National High School. The school provides secondary education and aims to support students’ academic development and personal growth.

Mission

The Department of Education is committed to protecting and promoting every Filipino’s right to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education. It aims to provide a child-friendly, safe, and motivating learning environment, support teachers, and encourage families and communities to participate in education.
